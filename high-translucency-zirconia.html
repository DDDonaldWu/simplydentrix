High-Translucency Zirconia

Our High-Translucency Zirconia restorations offer the ultimate balance of aesthetics and strength. Engineered with a higher yttria content, this material provides a level of translucency that rivals natural dentition for seamless blending in the aesthetic zone. While it mimics the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel, it still retains the flexural strength needed for multi-unit bridges and posterior load-bearing applications.
